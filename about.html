Find the right AI for the work.

BDX AI HUB is an independent, free-first directory built to help people discover and compare AI products across coding, app building, design, image, video, research, writing, audio and automation.

How recommendations work

The task finder uses a curated shortlist for each workflow. It is an editorial starting point, not a live benchmark or a claim that one tool is universally best.

Free-plan transparency

Free labels mean that a free plan, free access path or open-source option has been listed. They do not mean unlimited use. Credits, limits, commercial rights, availability and supported models can change. Verify the provider’s own plan page before choosing a tool.

Independence

BDX AI HUB is not affiliated with the listed AI providers. Names and marks belong to their respective owners.
