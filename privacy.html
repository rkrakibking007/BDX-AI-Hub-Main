Privacy, plainly.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What this website stores

BDX AI HUB is a static directory. Your saved tools, comparison shortlist and basic interface preferences are stored in your browser using local storage. They are not sent to a BDX AI HUB account because this release has no account system.

Notifications

The notification panel can request browser permission and send a local test notification. Background push notifications, email alerts and server-side notification subscriptions are not configured.

External AI services

When you follow an external provider link, that provider’s own privacy policy and terms apply. Do not submit confidential information to an AI provider unless you have reviewed its data practices.

Analytics and advertising

This starter release does not include analytics, advertising trackers or third-party marketing scripts.

Changes and contact

This notice should be reviewed if analytics, accounts, feedback forms or server-side features are added. A public contact address has not yet been configured.
